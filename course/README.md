# INFO 557 activity selection

The seven-week route selects 13 of the 38 activities in this repository. Each
week offers a short optional check connected to an existing objective or HAR
project task. It is not an added assessment. Students may stop at the stated
check without completing every stage or earning a badge. Week 7.5 adds no work.

| Week | Activities | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0; 6.2.3 | Forward prediction and choice of output units |
| 2 | 6.5.2; 8.3 | Local derivatives and optimizer update arithmetic |
| 3 | 7.1; 7.8 | Regularization mechanisms and checkpoint selection |
| 4 | 9.1; 9.3 | Shared filters and output-size reasoning |
| 5 | 10.1.2; 10.TF | Recurrent state and position information |
| 6 | 11.1; 11.GA | Metric trade-offs and bounded attribution claims |
| 7 | 11.3 | Diagnose existing curves and synthesize results |

Graph networks, distillation, transfer learning, extensive recurrent variants,
region-count theory and Hyperband remain in the full optional catalog. They
are not required by this compressed course. Early-stopping split controls are
a separate toy example; the participant-separated course split stays fixed.
Image convolution illustrates the mechanism; the HAR candidate uses 1D signals.
The clinical metric example is synthetic instruction, not medical guidance.

The source route is `weekly-activities.json`; `scripts/build_pages.py` validates
its IDs and renders `docs/course.html`. The course-local D2L integration copies
this reviewed route and inserts an optional practice section into the existing
weekly overviews, retaining reading support and native assessment links.

Review basis: current source and generated artifacts, the inherited
`analysis/activity_review.md`, independent calculation/regression checks, all
38 source-aware browser completion paths, and focused desktop/mobile inspection.
Source-aware browser checks use public toy answer handlers; they are not an
independent proof of every answer or evidence of student learning outcomes.
No new rubric, deadline, student-data collection or extra model run is added.
